# Recipe_Scale
I built this recipe scaling app after testing how ingredient changes affect recipes. When you adjust servings, I change amounts for items like yeast, baking powder, and seasonings with specific rules. The app includes weight and volume conversions, pan size calculations, and ingredient formatting tools to keep recipes clear and easy to edit.

---

**Live Demo:** [jovin-jijo.github.io/Recipe_Scale](https://jovin-jijo.github.io/Recipe_Scale/#pan)

---

## Features

- **Recipe Scaling (`#scale`):** Adjusts ingredient amounts instantly by target servings or scaling factor.
- **Unit Conversion (`#convert`):** Switch easily between grams, ounces, cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, milliliters, and pounds.
- **Baking Pan Converter (`#pan`):** Calculate surface area differences between round, square, and rectangular pans to keep your bake times and ratios accurate.
